Technology interests me most when it solves something real.

02 / Selected Work

Projects with a practical purpose.

These projects represent my current experience applying web technologies to real needs. The descriptions focus on the problem, my approach, and what I am learning.

Education · Web application

Tawjihi Smart Platform

A student-focused platform designed to make Tawjihi score calculations and academic information easier to navigate.

Problem: Students may find it difficult to work through score calculations and changing academic requirements.

Approach: Built a responsive web interface with client-side score-calculation logic and structured information.

What I learned: Translating rules into code requires careful handling of edge cases, accurate calculations, and clear feedback.

Local business · Digital presence

Clinic Digital Transformation

A practical effort to make a local dental clinic easier to find and contact online.

Problem: Important clinic information and contact options needed a clearer digital path for patients.

Approach: Created a lightweight website and connected digital touchpoints such as Maps, WhatsApp, and QR-based access.

What I learned: A useful digital solution depends on clarity, accessibility, and how easily people can act on the information.
